Frequently asked

Everything you'd reasonably ask.

Short answers. If you need more, the full How-To page has step-by-step screenshots.

How do I pay for a subscription?
We accept card (Visa, Verve, Mastercard), bank transfer, and Paystack. Payments are processed securely; you'll get an SMS receipt and your subscription activates instantly. Step-by-step →
How do giveaway entries work?
Every purchase — single article or subscription — auto-enters you into that month's draw. Premium gets 5× entries, Elite gets 15×. The draw is broadcast live on the platform every month.
How do I claim a reward if I win?
If your registered phone number is drawn, we call you within 24 hours to verify your details. Payouts under ₦1M land in your bank within 48 hours. Physical prizes (phones, laptops) ship via courier to the address you confirm.
How does the referral payout actually work?
You get a unique code at sign-up. Anyone who subscribes using it is linked to you for 12 months. You earn ₦100 on their first subscription, then ₦50 on every renewal in that window. Paid out automatically every 1st of the month.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes — anytime, from your dashboard. You keep access until the end of your paid period, and any pending giveaway entries still count.
Is FortuneGate available outside Nigeria?
Right now the platform is NGN-only and most content is Nigeria-context. We're rolling out Ghana and Kenya in Q3 2026. Sign up to be notified.
