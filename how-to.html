Section 01

Sign up and verify your phone.

Two short forms. We split account details from phone verification so a lost device never locks you out of your account permanently.

1

Fill in your details

First name, surname, email, password (8+ characters), and the age confirmation checkbox. Referral code is optional — if a friend gave you one, this is where it goes.

Required~45 sec
2

Add your phone

Enter your Nigerian phone number. This becomes both your login and the destination for any payouts you earn — so make sure it's reachable.

Required+234 prefix
3

Enter your OTP

We text you a 6-digit code. Type it in — most phones autofill from the SMS. If you don't receive it in 60 seconds you can resend or switch to email verification.

SMS or email fallback
✓

Pick your tier

Free, Reader, Premium or Elite. You can change anytime — Premium and above unlock 5× and 15× giveaway entries automatically.

Cancel anytime
What if I lose my phone? Email us at hello@fortunegate.ng from the email on your account. We re-verify identity then update your phone — payouts pause until that's done.

1

Pick a plan or a single article

From the homepage or any locked article. You'll see the NGN price (₦5,000/mo for Premium, ₦200 per single article) with no add-on fees at checkout.

2

Choose a payment method

Card, bank transfer, USSD, Paystack or Monnify. The selected method is highlighted in gold. If your card was already saved, you can pay with one tap.

3

Confirm & get your SMS receipt

The success screen shows your unlocked content immediately. You'll also receive an SMS to your registered phone with the transaction ID, plan, and a link to download a PDF receipt.

Refunds: Single articles are non-refundable once unlocked. Subscriptions can be refunded within 7 days of payment if you've consumed less than 3 articles — contact us at hello@fortunegate.ng with your transaction ID.
Section 03

How to claim a reward if you win.

Every month's draw is broadcast live and winners are notified by phone within 24 hours. Payouts under ₦1M land in your bank in 48 hours. Physical prizes ship via courier.

1

We call you within 24 hours

The phone number you used to sign up is the one we call. We confirm your full name, the prize won, and ask you to read a 4-digit confirmation code we sent by SMS.

9am–7pm WAT3 attempts
2

Verify on claim.html

For cash payouts: bank name, account number, account name (must match the name on file). For physical prizes: delivery address and a confirmation phone number.

Form takes ~2 min
3

We pay out within 48 hours

Cash winnings are credited to your verified bank account by NIBSS transfer. Physical prizes ship by GIG Logistics or DHL depending on item value — you'll get a tracking SMS.

NIBSS transferPublic log
Heads up: We never ask for processing fees, bank PINs, or to "activate" your prize. Every legitimate FortuneGate call comes from +234 800 000 0000. Anything else is a scam.
